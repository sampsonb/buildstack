# @itsbuildstack voice

Read this file before drafting, scheduling, or posting anything from [X @itsbuildstack](https://x.com/itsbuildstack).

The account posts only on a day with a verified notable price change or a notable new tool, at most one post a day. The tone is plain and factual: what changed or launched, the date, a verified figure if we have one, and a link. Nothing goes live until Sampson approves the exact text. Do not reply, like, follow, or send DMs from this account.

Dates below are in America/Chicago (CT).

## NEVER USE

- Em dashes or en dashes
- Hashtags
- Emoji
- Arrows
- Hype words: game-changer, unlock, dive in, revolutionary, best ever
- The line "It's not X, it's Y" (or close variations)
- Superlatives without data (best, top, leading, number one, unless a dated BuildStack rank or a counted figure backs it)
- Any price or claim that was not verified on an official page that day

Older posts that break these rules (hashtags, thread emoji, daily tips) are not the current voice. Since 21 Nov 2026 the account does not post daily tips.

## Checklist before every post

1. Read this file.
2. Confirm today has a verified notable price change or a notable new tool, checked on an official page today.
3. Confirm @itsbuildstack has not already posted today. If it has, stop.
4. Every price, date, and claim in the draft appears on that official page (or on our dated price-change log built from one). If it does not, cut it.
5. The draft includes a date and a link (the BuildStack tool page, /price-changes, or the official page).
6. Scan the draft against the NEVER USE list.
7. Show Sampson the exact post text and wait for a yes on that wording. Do not post, schedule, like, reply, follow, or DM.
